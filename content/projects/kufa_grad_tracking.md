# Kufa Grad Tracking Platform
The University of Kufa uses this platform to follow its graduates and connect them with jobs. I have built and maintained it since 2023, and I wrote most of the code.

## What I built

- A Go API with clean architecture: graduate records, job matching, notifications, chat, scheduled jobs, and translations.
- A Next.js web app in Arabic and English, with translations managed in Crowdin and deployed to Cloudflare.
- A QA portal (FastAPI and React) where testers get assigned test runs and report results.
- Error tracking with Sentry, and end-to-end tests with Playwright.

## Stack

Go, PostgreSQL, Next.js, React, TanStack Query, next-intl, FastAPI, Sentry, Playwright, Vitest, Docker.
