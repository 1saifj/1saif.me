Najaf Guide is a city guide for visitors to Najaf. It is in progress and not yet public.

## What I am building

- A NestJS API with domain-driven design, Drizzle, Better Auth with Redis sessions, and New Relic monitoring.
- A Flutter app with clean architecture, BLoC and Firebase, including a notification inbox.
- A Next.js web app and landing page in Arabic and English, deployed to Cloudflare.
- Translation files published as open source on GitHub.

## Stack

NestJS, PostgreSQL, Drizzle, Redis, Flutter, Next.js, next-intl, Cloudflare.
