# Kufa Hackathon Platform
This platform runs the University of Kufa hackathon. I wrote all of it.

## What it does

- Team sign-up and confirmation.
- Project submissions, with a submission banner on the team dashboard.
- Judge assignment.
- Notifications that combine Supabase realtime with Firebase Cloud Messaging.
- Bulk email to participants from Supabase functions.

## Stack

Next.js, Supabase, Tailwind CSS, TanStack Query, Jest, Playwright. The repository has 127 test files.
